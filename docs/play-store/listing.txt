App name:
Japa Siddhi

Short description:
Daily japa, today’s panchangam, Annadanam and temple seva with Bilva Patra Trust.

Full description:
Japa Siddhi is a spiritual companion from Bilva Patra Trust, Visakhapatnam. It helps devotees chant daily japa, follow today’s panchangam, and take part in Annadanam, Baanalingam, and Nithya Homam seva.

Japa
• Start community or private japa with a mantra and a daily goal
• Track today’s count, streak, and lifetime progress
• Join japa challenges and view your analytics

Panchangam
• See today’s nakshatra, tithi, vara, paksha, yoga, and karana
• Follow upcoming festivals such as Janmashtami and Navaratri

Seva
• Offer Annadanam, apply for Baanalingam, or enroll in Nithya Homam
• Voluntary offerings are paid in your UPI app (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, or any UPI). They are donations to Bilva Patra Trust, not Google Play purchases
• Track orders and delivery from the Orders tab

Account
• Sign in with your mobile number and email. A 4-digit OTP is sent to your email
• Choose your language and keep spiritual details such as gothram and nakshatram
• Get help from Customer Care, or delete your account from Settings
